Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Purpose and access

PlotPuls is a private tool used by the channel operator to manage owner-approved YouTube Shorts. It is not offered as a service for visitors or outside users.

Google user data

With the operator's authorization, the automation accesses the operator's Google Drive files needed for the workflow and uses the YouTube API to upload approved videos and set their permitted privacy or scheduling settings. A separate Google Apps Script within the operator's account processes approved Gmail messages and saves their video and metadata attachments to Google Drive. The workflow does not use these permissions to collect information from website visitors.

Storage and processing

Approved video files and metadata may be stored in the operator's Gmail and Google Drive account. Upload status and technical logs may be held in the operator's private GitHub repository or GitHub Actions logs. Videos uploaded to YouTube are handled according to YouTube's settings and policies. Google and GitHub process information as infrastructure providers for these operations. Data is retained until deleted by the operator or according to the applicable providers' retention policies.

Sharing and use

Google user data accessed by the workflow is used only to support the operator-authorized video upload process. It is not sold, used for advertising, or provided to unrelated third parties. Access credentials are kept outside this public website.

Control and deletion

The operator can remove submitted content from Google Drive or YouTube, disable the automation, and revoke the app's Google access in their Google Account security settings. Requests relating to this private workflow can be directed to its operator through the associated GitHub account.

Changes

This page will be updated if the workflow's handling of Google user data changes.
