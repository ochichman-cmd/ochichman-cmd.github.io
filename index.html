Independent creator workflow

PlotPuls

PlotPuls creates short animated stories for YouTube. A private automation workflow helps the channel operator transfer approved video files and metadata to YouTube.

How the workflow works

Once the operator approves a video, the video and its metadata are transferred through the operator's Google account, temporarily stored in Google Drive and processed by a private GitHub Actions workflow. The workflow uses Google authorization to upload the approved video to the operator's YouTube channel, either privately or with a specified schedule.

This is an operator-only tool, not a public app. It does not offer Google account access to visitors.
